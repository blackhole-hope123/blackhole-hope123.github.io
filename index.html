Rare-Event Detection with Temporal Graph Neural Networks

Developed temporal and graph-based representations for 9.5M transactions with ~0.1% positive examples. Compared XGBoost, Transformer, and Temporal GNN approaches and used imbalance-aware training to improve detection of rare events. The Temporal GNN achieved 0.851 PR-AUC and reduced false negatives by 30%.

PyTorch · Temporal GNNs · Transformers · XGBoost · Graph ML · Imbalanced Learning
