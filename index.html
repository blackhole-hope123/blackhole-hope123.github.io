Parkinson's Disease Progression Modeling

Role: Data Scientist Data: AMP PD longitudinal data Result: 55% lower MAE vs. baseline

Research Question

Can longitudinal clinical measurements improve prediction of Parkinson's disease progression, and do additional protein-derived features provide useful predictive signal?

Approach

Developed lagged predictive models and analyzed patient progression patterns using longitudinal biomedical data. I also systematically evaluated whether protein-derived features provided additional predictive information.

Finding

Reduced MAE by 55% relative to baseline. Under the tested setup, protein-derived features provided limited incremental predictive signal.

Rare-Event Detection with Temporal Graph Neural Networks

Role: Data Scientist Data: 9.5M transactions · ~0.1% positive Result: 0.851 PR-AUC · 0.81 recall

Problem

Detect money-laundering transactions under extreme class imbalance, where positive examples account for only about 0.1% of the data and conventional accuracy is not an informative evaluation metric.

Approach

Compared XGBoost, Transformer, and Temporal GNN approaches and incorporated imbalance-aware training, including focal loss and resampling, to improve rare-event detection.

Result

The Temporal GNN achieved 0.851 PR-AUC and 0.81 recall, compared with 0.48 recall for the XGBoost baseline.
